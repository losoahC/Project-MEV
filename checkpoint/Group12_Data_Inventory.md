# FT565 Group 12 | MEV data inventory

This inventory traces three measurements to their inputs: the price paid for ETH by the labeled wallets, the cash flow of nearby trading strategies, and the fees paid to block producers. All local links point to the project workspace and are relative to this Markdown file.

**Scope.** The wallet-history cutoff is Ethereum mainnet block **26,349,764** (November 5, 2026, 16:15:35 UTC). The continuous block sample covers **26,300,640 through 26,303,950**, inclusive. Later blocks were collected selectively. The purchase ledger uses the 16 labeled wallets. The broader exchange and strategy search uses 55 Ethereum addresses. These samples have different denominators.

## 3. Join keys, units and a worked example

- **Identity and order.** Join receipts and traces to transactions by hash. Within each block, order transactions by transaction index and events by log index. Preserve the wallet address when one hash affects multiple wallets. Identify tokens by chain and contract address.
- **Amounts and missing values.** Ethereum RPC quantities commonly use hexadecimal strings. Convert native ETH from wei using 18 decimals. Apply each token's contract decimals to its raw integer amount. Derived ledgers often store decimal quantities as strings to preserve precision. A missing price is recorded as JSON `null`.
- **Worked example.** Wallet 10 paid 10,000,000 USDT and received 3,552.228717651589845452 ETH/WETH. The reference prices were USD 0.99876 per USDT and USD 2,248.40 per ETH. The ledger computes overpayment as payment value minus the reference value of ETH received, or **USD 2,000,768.95**, excluding gas. Measuring how much a strategy earned from that trade requires the execution and cash-flow records above.
